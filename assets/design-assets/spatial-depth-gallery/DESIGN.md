# Atmospheric Depth Gallery

Author: Houmahani Kane

## Visual design
Five botanical images occupy alternating positions along the camera’s depth path. Editorial color-chip labels remain readable beside the active flower. The background borrows the image palette, mixes two soft colored fields and changes mood through the passage; scroll velocity drives a restrained pulse and a light trail. The supplied still shows a warm coral flower against a muted blue/purple depth composition, with microtype rather than oversized display lettering.

## Production use
Editorial storytelling, artistic portfolios, color systems and image-led case studies. The distinctive idea is that each image authors its surrounding atmosphere and transitions through camera depth, not a flat carousel.

## Interaction
Native wheel and touch depth scrolling, bounded camera travel, pointer parallax, velocity-reactive trail, and D-key debug panel retained. No synthetic wheel sequence is introduced.

## Evidence boundary
Author still/video pixels were inspected; the local adapter was not rendered. No claim of visual equivalence, runtime acceptance or user approval is made.
