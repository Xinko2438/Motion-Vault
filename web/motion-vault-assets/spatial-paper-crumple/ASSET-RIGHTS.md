# Asset rights and limits

- Source explicitly calls the seven fictional brand SVGs self-made copyright-free data; repository includes their originals under MIT.
- FBX/EXR are the project’s author-produced Houdini exports, distributed with the MIT repository.
- Caveat and Yomogi are bundled unchanged from Google Fonts under SIL OFL, with full notices and download hashes.
- Source video stills are evidence of the author demo only, never labeled as recordings of the local adapter.

Code MIT is documented in source/LICENSE. Third-party images, fonts and HDR environment are tracked separately. Source snapshots are study material; production teams must check the specified caveats before shipping.
