# Asset rights and limits

- README attributes the five bundled images to Lummi.ai.
- Lummi’s current license permits copying, editing and website/template use; prohibits image resale and bundling a competing stock-image service. https://www.lummi.ai/license verified 2026-10-07.
- Individual Lummi item IDs and creators are absent upstream; preserve this uncertainty, do not claim photo authorship or a per-image release.
- Editorial color-chip inspiration is credited to @thisislandscape, as in upstream.

Code MIT is documented in source/LICENSE. Third-party images, fonts and HDR environment are tracked separately. Source snapshots are study material; production teams must check the specified caveats before shipping.
