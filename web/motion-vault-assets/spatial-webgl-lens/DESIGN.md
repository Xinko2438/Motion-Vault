# Refraction through an editorial composition

Use for studio/portfolio introductions, premium campaign pages and expressive editorial storytelling. The reference is the whole composition: typography and imagery aligned in DOM/WebGL, a damped physical lens, restrained image transformations on scroll and a spatial image-cube reveal.

Preserve the author's lens geometry, refractive index, thickness, chromatic aberration, distortion and camera scale. Preserve the real Poppins/Playfair glyph relationship between DOM and WebGL; system-font replacement would change the shape seen through the lens.

The local source keeps those elements and the native effects toggle. No local render has been visually reviewed yet. Judge typography, lens surface, motion settling, scroll continuity and GPU cost before approving production use.
